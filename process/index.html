How it works

Four steps, no surprises

You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to achieve. You leave with a plan whether or not you hire us.

  2. 02

    Proposal & design

    A fixed quote and timeline, then design concepts for your key pages. We iterate until you're genuinely happy — no build starts before that.

  3. 03

    Build & review

    We build on a private staging link you can check any time. Weekly progress notes, and one round of revisions baked into the price.

  4. 04

    Launch & support

    We handle domains, hosting, analytics and redirects. After launch you get a walkthrough video and 30 days of free support.
