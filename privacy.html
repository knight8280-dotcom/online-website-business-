Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This site is the marketing website for Knight Web Studio. We collect as little information about you as we can get away with, and this page explains exactly what is collected, by whom, and why. No analytics or advertising trackers run on this site.

The contact form

When you send an enquiry, the details you type — your name, email address, business name, budget range, the services you tick and your message — are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a form relay service, which forwards them to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes your submission solely to deliver it; its handling of that data is described in the Web3Forms privacy policy.

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry and for no other purpose. We never sell your details, share them with third parties for marketing, or add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for. Enquiry emails are kept as ordinary business correspondence; ask and we will delete yours.

Spam protection (hCaptcha)

The contact form is protected by hCaptcha, which loads only when you scroll near the form or interact with it — not on page load. hCaptcha may set cookies and collect technical information (such as your IP address and browser characteristics) to tell people from bots, under the hCaptcha privacy policy and terms. If you'd rather not trigger it, you can email us directly instead at knightwebsitesllc@gmail.com.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard technical logs (including your IP address) when pages and assets are requested — see the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive or review those logs.

What stays on your device

  • Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.
  • Fonts are served from this site directly — no requests go to Google Fonts or any other font network.

Links to other sites

Our portfolio links out to client websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and similar laws), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. For anything sent through this site, that information is limited to your enquiry email — write to knightwebsitesllc@gmail.com and we'll act on it promptly.

Changes

If what this site collects ever changes — for example, if we add privacy-respecting analytics — this page will be updated first and the date at the top revised.
